# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the TestHistory community a respectful, inclusive, and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, ability, experience,
or level of contribution.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure;
- helping make discussions accessible to people with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate intimidation;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- pressuring others to disclose personal, employer, or security-sensitive information;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, security reports, and
other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository maintainer. If a concern also involves a
vulnerability or sensitive technical information, use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish private evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers will protect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible. Anyone
handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and step aside when necessary.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may apply measures proportionate to the behavior and its impact:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation and request to change behavior.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited restriction on project participation.
4. **Permanent restriction** — removal from project spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Decisions should be documented privately, applied consistently, and communicated to affected people
without exposing confidential details.
